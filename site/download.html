Download APK All releases
  1. Open the file you downloaded.

    Android asks once whether your browser may install apps. Allow it, then tap Install.

  2. Make Yoke your home app.

    Press the home button and choose Yoke, or go to Settings, Apps, Default apps, Home app.

  3. Point it at your Obsidian vault.

    Swipe left, tap "Pick vault folder" and choose the folder Syncthing keeps in sync. Yoke finds your Cockpit board's columns there.

  4. Turn on double tap to lock, if you want it.

    Locking uses Android's accessibility service and nothing else. Because Yoke isn't installed from a store, Android 13 and newer first need App info, the three-dot menu, "Allow restricted settings".

Smart grayscale (optional)

Smart grayscale turns the whole phone grayscale except in the apps you choose. It needs a one-time permission that Android only lets developer tools grant. The grant survives Yoke updates, and Yoke uses it only to switch colour correction on and off. Pick one way:

  1. On the phone, with Shizuku.

    Install Shizuku, start it with Wireless debugging, then open Yoke, Settings, Smart grayscale and tap "Grant with Shizuku".

  2. From a computer, with adb.

    Connect the phone and run adb shell pm grant com.outsmartis.yoke android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS

Then turn on Yoke's accessibility service. On Android 13 and newer, first open App info, the three-dot menu, "Allow restricted settings".
